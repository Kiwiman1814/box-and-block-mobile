# Box and Block (mobile test)

A browser hand-rehab mini game. The phone camera tracks a hand marked with:

- a **green dot** in the middle of the palm, with a wide **black ring** around it
- **blue/purple** on all five fingertips

Make a fist over a cube to grab it, lift it over the wall, open your hand to drop it.
"Touch test mode" lets you try the game with your finger instead of the camera.

**Play:** https://kiwiman1814.github.io/box-and-block-mobile/

**v2 (no markers, MediaPipe hand tracking, 3 levels):** https://kiwiman1814.github.io/box-and-block-mobile/v2/

Everything runs in the browser; camera images never leave the phone.
